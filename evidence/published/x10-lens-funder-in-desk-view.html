The eleven checks

Which rules decided this

The debtor's billing country selects the United States profile, and with it: UPS, FedEx, USPS, DHL as the carriers this desk believes, a 5-digit ZIP prefix as the address, a delivery no more than 90 days before the invoice, a tenor no longer than 90 days, and Seller Protection as PayPal's cover.

Those numbers are configuration, one profile per country; the eleven checks, the score and the price are the same everywhere. A United States debtor's receivable is invoiced and settled in US dollars in this build.

✓
E00 Sandbox override: PayPal returned UNKNOWN for the sandbox account in use. The check is recorded as overridden, not passed.
A PayPal transaction underlies the sale, Seller Protection is ELIGIBLE for it, and the tracking number is registered against it. Where no PayPal transaction exists the status is recorded as NOT_ON_RAIL and the desk proceeds on the documents and the debtor's history alone, priced one band lower. These programmes cover eligible purchases rather than services, so an ineligibility means the rail declines this transaction — never that the goods behind the invoice are worthless.
